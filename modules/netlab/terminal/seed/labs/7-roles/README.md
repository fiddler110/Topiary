# Lab A11: Roles and structure (stretch)

Refactor the playbooks into `web` and `firewall` roles without changing behaviour.

The full walkthrough, with every command explained, is the lab page **Lab A11** in the lab reader (the lab index
in your portal). This folder is where you do the work.

- You fill in: `labs/7-roles/` (`site.yml` and `roles/`)
- Work from `~/netlab`, on branch **a** (`*-a-@POD@`).
- Check your work: `dojo netlab check A11`
- Stuck or wrecked the firewall or web node? `ansible-playbook reset_fw.yml -e branch=a`
